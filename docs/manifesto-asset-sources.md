## Original project-created material

The folio, illustrative lifecycle site, wind instrument, folding schematic network, transition cues, procedural paper/concrete/metal/fabric maps, integration controls and generator source were created for this Alidaunia handoff. ## Brand artwork

The logo source is the current supplied repository's `public/brand/alidaunia-logo.svg`:
https://github.com/Ukendiran-M/Alidaunia-3D

`alidaunia-logo-source.svg` is the supplied artwork. `m6-logo-transparent.svg` removes the source's white background rectangle, makes existing fill colours explicit and preserves the vector paths/white details. The M6 model is a shallow extrusion of those paths, with small bevels and authored registration transforms. No new brand identity was designed. Brand and trademark rights remain with the respective owner.
